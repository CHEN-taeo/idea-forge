# Idea Forge — Product Brief
> Version 1.0 · May 31, 2026

## What We're Building

**Idea Forge turns meetings into structured decision sessions.**

One screen in the room. Everyone else on their phone. Silent parallel ideation → shared reveal → structured debate → commitments someone owns.

Not a game. Not a whiteboard. A decision-making tool that uses game mechanics to solve the human problems that kill meetings.

## The Problem

Meetings fail for three human reasons, not technical ones:

1. **The loud person dominates.** One voice fills the room while quieter people with better ideas stay silent.
2. **No structure creates drift.** Without phases, time pressure, and constraints, discussions circle without converging.
3. **Nothing gets decided.** People leave with vague feelings, not commitments. Nobody knows who's doing what by when.

Existing tools don't solve this. Video calls give everyone a mute button but no reason to unmute. Whiteboard tools give everyone a cursor but no structure. Project management tools track tasks but don't generate them from discussion.

## Who It's For

**Primary: Team leads and facilitators** who run regular meetings — sprint retros, quarterly planning, design sprints, prioritization sessions. They need structure, equal participation, and actionable output.

**Secondary: Their teams** — 3 to 8 people who join on their phones. They need low friction, psychological safety, and a reason to speak.

**Not for:** Solo brainstorming, casual chat, large all-hands (100+ people).

## Core Differentiators

| What we do | Why it matters |
|------------|---------------|
| **Parallel silent ideation** on phones, revealed simultaneously | The quiet person can't hide. The loud person can't dominate. Quantity and quality both go up. |
| **Structured phases** with time pressure | Divergence → convergence → stress-testing → decision. No drift. |
| **Roles that create permission** | "I'm the Contrarian, I'm supposed to challenge this." Roles give people a reason to speak. |
| **Commitment ceremony** at the end | Every session produces named action items with deadlines. The output is a decision, not just ideas. |
| **Zero setup** | No accounts, no invites, no installs. Host creates a room, QR code on screen, everyone scans and joins. |

## Success Metrics

| Metric | What good looks like |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Participation equality** | Every player submits at least one idea per round. No single player dominates idea count. |
| **Session completion** | Team reaches the commitment ceremony. Someone leaves with a named action item. |
| **Return rate** | A facilitator who uses it once uses it again within 2 weeks. |
| **Time to first idea** | Under 3 minutes from room creation to first idea submitted. |

## What We're NOT

- **Not a game** — scores exist to create momentum, not to declare winners. The leaderboard is secondary to the commitments.
- **Not a project management tool** — we generate action items, we don't track them. Integration with Notion/Linear is the handoff point.
- **Not a video meeting replacement** — we assume people are in the same room. The screen is shared physically, not via screen share.

## Design Principles

1. **The screen is the anchor.** Everything visible on the shared screen should be readable from across a room. Big text, high contrast, minimal clutter.
2. **The phone is the input device.** Mobile-first for everyone except the facilitator. Touch targets, simplified layouts, one action at a time.
3. **Structure should feel like scaffolding, not a cage.** Phases guide but don't dictate. The facilitator can override timing. The flow adapts to the room's energy.
4. **Output over ceremony.** The session report and commitments matter more than the leaderboard. The trophy screen is 10% celebration, 90% action planning.
